Privacy Policy

How this website collects, uses, and protects your information.

About This Website

This website is owned and operated by Neelam Shahzadi, AI Local SEO, GEO, and AEO Specialist. The website is available at neelamseoconsulting.com and is used to provide information about SEO services, share resources, and invite enquiries from prospective clients.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, you can contact Neelam directly at hello@neelamseoconsulting.com.

What Data Is Collected

When you use this website or contact Neelam through it, the following information may be collected depending on how you interact with the site:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your website URL
  • Your target location or business location
  • The content of any message you send through a contact form
  • Calendly booking details if you book a call, including your name, email, and any notes you provide
  • Basic website usage data if analytics tools are added to this site in the future

Why Data Is Collected

Any data collected through this website is used only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry in a timely and relevant way
  • To review your website's SEO needs before or during a consultation
  • To book, confirm, and manage discovery or strategy calls
  • To improve the content, structure, and performance of this website over time

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Tools That May Process Your Data

When you interact with this website, your data may be processed by the following third-party tools and services:

  • Gmail or email provider — used to receive and respond to messages sent through contact forms or directly by email
  • Calendly — used to manage call bookings; Calendly's own privacy policy applies to any data you submit when booking
  • Website hosting provider — the server that hosts this website may log basic access information such as IP addresses and pages visited
  • Google Analytics or Microsoft Clarity — these tools may be added to this website in the future to help understand visitor behaviour; if added, a cookie notice will be displayed and this policy will be updated

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to any personal data held about you, to ask for corrections if any information is inaccurate, or to request that your data is deleted. To exercise any of these rights, please email hello@neelamseoconsulting.com with your request.

Requests will be handled promptly and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions and email correspondence are retained only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and manage any ongoing work. You can request deletion at any time by contacting hello@neelamseoconsulting.com.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date below.
