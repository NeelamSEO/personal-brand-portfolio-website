Cookie Policy

How cookies are used on this website and how you can control them.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, remember preferences, and collect basic usage information to help improve the site over time.

How This Website Uses Cookies

This website may use cookies in the following ways:

Essential Cookies

Some cookies are necessary for the website to function correctly. These include cookies that support the sticky navigation header and basic browser compatibility. These cookies do not track you and do not require consent.

Calendly Embed

This website includes an embedded Calendly booking calendar on the Contact page. When you interact with the Calendly embed, Calendly may set its own cookies to support the booking functionality and remember your preferences. These cookies are set by Calendly and are subject to Calendly's own privacy and cookie policy.

Analytics and Performance Cookies

Analytics tools such as Google Analytics (GA4) or Microsoft Clarity are not currently active on this website. If they are added in the future, they will be used to understand how visitors use the site — such as which pages are visited, how long visitors stay, and how they navigate between pages. These tools set non-essential cookies and will only be activated after a proper cookie consent mechanism is in place. This Cookie Policy will be updated at that time.

Cookies Set by Third Parties

Third-party services embedded or linked from this website, such as Calendly, may set their own cookies independently. This website does not control those cookies. Please refer to the relevant third-party privacy policies for more information.

How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View which cookies are stored on your device
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Delete cookies already stored on your device
  • Set preferences to block all cookies by default

Please note that blocking certain cookies may affect how this website functions. For guidance on managing cookies, visit your browser's help documentation or a resource such as allaboutcookies.org.

Changes to This Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated as new tools are added to this website. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. If you have questions, contact hello@neelamseoconsulting.com.
