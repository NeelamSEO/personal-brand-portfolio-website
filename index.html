The SEO System

A local SEO system built for how buyers search now

Buyers validate across multiple touchpoints before they enquire.

This is how local buyers decide

01
Google Search

Search

They look for your service in their location. If you are not there, you do not exist to them.

Buyers type your service and city into Google. Your page must appear before a competitor's does.
02
Google Maps

Maps

They compare businesses on Google Maps. Missing from the top three means missing the call.

Maps listings get the majority of local clicks. An incomplete or unoptimised GBP loses those calls to competitors.
03
Reviews

Reviews

They check ratings and feedback. A low count or poor score sends them straight to a competitor.

Buyers read reviews before deciding. Low review counts or poor ratings send them to a competitor immediately.
04
Your Website

Website

They confirm credibility and clarity. A weak page loses the deal your rankings earned.

After Maps and reviews, buyers visit your site. If it does not clearly answer their question, they leave.
05
Direct Call

Decision

They choose who to contact. The business visible across every step gets chosen.

Every step either builds or loses trust. Businesses that show up well across all touchpoints get chosen. Others get ignored.
