SEO service questions from business owners

What SEO services do you offer for service businesses?

I offer Local SEO, Google Business Profile SEO, GEO, AEO, technical SEO audits, service page SEO, local landing page strategy, and SEO content strategy.

Do you offer Google Business Profile SEO?

Yes. I review categories, services, descriptions, photos, posts, reviews, Q&A, local relevance, and lead actions.

What is GEO in SEO?

GEO means Generative Engine Optimization. It helps AI platforms understand your brand, services, locations, and expertise so your business becomes easier to cite and recommend.

What is AEO?

AEO means Answer Engine Optimization. It structures content to answer buyer questions clearly for AI Overviews, featured snippets, voice search, and answer engines.

Do you create service pages and location pages?

Yes. I help plan, structure, and optimize service pages and location pages with buyer intent, local relevance, FAQs, trust signals, and internal links.
