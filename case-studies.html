SEO Case Study Frameworks for Service Businesses

Example SEO project frameworks showing how I approach Local SEO, Google Business Profile SEO, GEO, AEO, and technical SEO challenges for service-based businesses.

Local SEO

Local Service Business SEO Visibility Growth

A service business in the UK was getting some organic traffic but very few enquiries. Rankings were inconsistent across service terms and the GBP profile was generating almost no clicks or calls.

IndustryLocal Service Business
MarketUnited Kingdom
FocusLocal SEO + GBP

Challenge

The website had service pages that were generic and not targeting local buyer intent. The Google Business Profile was incomplete with wrong categories, no service listings, and no consistent post activity. Local citations had inconsistent NAP data causing trust issues.

Strategy

Full local SEO audit, followed by a restructure of service pages with city-level intent keywords, a complete GBP rebuild, citation cleanup, and a review acquisition system. Location pages were created for each target city with unique content and internal links.

Actions

  • Keyword research for service + city combinations
  • Rewrote 8 service pages with buyer intent structure
  • Created 6 location pages with unique local content
  • Rebuilt GBP profile with correct categories and 14 service listings
  • Cleaned up 40+ citation inconsistencies
  • Set up GBP post schedule and review request system

Outcome

Improved local pack visibility for primary service + city searches. GBP click-throughs and call actions increased. More consistent enquiry flow from organic and map pack results within 90 days.

GBP Optimization

Google Business Profile Optimization Project

A multi-location service business in Canada had GBP profiles set up for each location but they were underperforming with low visibility, minimal engagement, and almost no inbound calls from Maps.

IndustryMulti-location Service
MarketCanada
FocusGBP SEO

Challenge

Multiple GBP profiles had inconsistent information, wrong primary categories, no service listings, thin descriptions, and no photo strategy. Review responses were absent and Q&A sections were unanswered. Profiles were not appearing in the local 3-pack for target searches.

Strategy

GBP audit across all locations followed by a systematic optimization of each profile — categories, services, photos, descriptions, posts, and review response strategy. A consistent posting cadence was established with service-focused and location-specific content.

Actions

  • Audited and corrected all category and attribute settings
  • Added 12–18 service listings per location
  • Wrote optimized business descriptions with local keywords
  • Uploaded 30+ optimized photos per location
  • Set up Q&A with 10 pre-populated answers per profile
  • Created GBP post templates and 30-day post calendar
  • Built review request email and SMS sequence

Outcome

Profiles began appearing in local 3-pack results for target search terms. Call actions and website clicks from GBP increased. Review count grew and profile engagement improved within 60 days.

AI Search + AEO

AI Search and AEO Content Structure Project

A professional services business in the US wanted to appear in AI Overviews and ChatGPT-style answers for their target queries. Their website had no structured FAQ content and no schema markup.

IndustryProfessional Services
MarketUnited States
FocusGEO + AEO

Challenge

The website had good content but it was not structured for AI search. No FAQ sections, no answer-first formatting, no schema markup, and no entity clarity. The brand was invisible in AI Overviews and not cited by any AI tool for their core service queries.

Strategy

GEO and AEO audit followed by content restructuring. Added FAQ sections to all key pages, created answer-first content blocks for high-value queries, implemented schema markup, and built entity clarity through consistent brand signals across the web.

Actions

  • Mapped 40+ buyer questions across service areas
  • Added FAQ sections with schema to 10 key pages
  • Restructured service page intros as direct answers
  • Implemented FAQ, LocalBusiness, and Service schema
  • Created brand entity signals across 15 authority platforms
  • Built author bio and expertise signals across site

Outcome

The brand began appearing in featured snippets for target FAQ queries. Initial citations appeared in AI Overviews within 60 days. Brand mentions in AI tools increased. Organic click-through rate improved on pages with answer-first structure.

Technical SEO

Technical SEO Cleanup and Roadmap Project

A service business in the UK had been publishing content regularly for 18 months but rankings had plateaued. Traffic was flat and enquiries were not growing despite ongoing blog work.

IndustryService Business
MarketUnited Kingdom
FocusTechnical SEO

Challenge

A crawl audit revealed 200+ pages with indexing issues, multiple redirect chains, duplicate content across location pages, broken internal links, and no schema markup. Core Web Vitals scores were failing on mobile. The content calendar was being wasted because the technical foundation was broken.

Strategy

Full technical SEO audit first, followed by a prioritized roadmap organized by impact and difficulty. High-impact technical fixes were implemented before any new content work began. The roadmap was shared with the internal team with clear timelines and effort estimates.

Actions

  • Full crawl audit and indexing review
  • Fixed 200+ pages with noindex, orphan, or duplicate issues
  • Cleaned up 4 redirect chains causing PageRank dilution
  • Fixed broken internal links across 60 pages
  • Implemented LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQ schema
  • Improved mobile Core Web Vitals scores
  • Delivered a 3-month SEO roadmap with prioritized action list

Outcome

Crawl coverage improved significantly. Rankings began to recover and new content started performing within 45 days of technical fixes. The team had a clear roadmap they could execute without additional SEO support needed on every task.
