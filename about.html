My Background

How I got into AI Local SEO

I work fully remotely with service businesses across the UK, US, and Canada. Engagements run via email, Slack, and regular video calls.

I started on the agency side, working on technical SEO and local search projects across multiple verticals. Early on I learned that most SEO problems are not technical mysteries. They are gaps between what a business offers, how buyers search for it, and how search engines interpret the signals on the page and profile.

Local search is where service business revenue actually moves. A cleaning company or a clinic does not need national rankings. It needs to appear when someone searches for that exact service in that city, and appear credibly. AI Overviews and answer engines have changed how local buyers find services. Most agencies still treat AI search as a separate experiment. It is already the buyer's first touchpoint.

Service businesses do not need more traffic. They need qualified enquiries from people who are ready to book. The strategy for that is different from ecommerce or content sites. It needs tight local signals, clear intent matching, and a profile and website that builds trust fast. That is what I focus on.

Frequently asked questions

I work with cleaning companies, clinics, finance and accounting firms, construction and home services, consultants, agencies, real estate, legal services, and B2B service providers. The common thread is local trust and qualified enquiries driving revenue.

Do you only work with UK clients?

No. I work with service businesses across the UK, United States, and Canada. The local search behavior is different in each market, and I tailor strategy accordingly. Everything is delivered remotely.

What is the difference between Local SEO, GEO, and AEO?

Local SEO ranks you in Google Search and Google Maps for service-plus-city queries. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) helps AI tools like ChatGPT and Perplexity understand and cite your brand. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) structures your content for featured snippets, AI Overviews, and voice search. They overlap but each has distinct levers.

How long does it take to see results?

Google Business Profile improvements often show movement within 30 to 60 days. Organic search results typically take 3 to 6 months to compound. AI search citations can appear within weeks once the content and schema are in place. SEO is not instant, but it does compound.

Do you offer one-off audits or ongoing retainers?

Both. Some clients only want the SEO Growth Audit and a clear action roadmap their team can execute. Others want me to handle implementation across content, GBP, technical SEO, and AI search visibility on an ongoing basis. We decide what fits during the discovery call.

What makes your approach different from a typical SEO agency?

I focus on qualified enquiries, not traffic numbers. I treat Local SEO, GBP, GEO, and AEO as one connected system, not separate services. You work with me directly, no account managers, no hand-offs. And I will tell you when a fix is not worth doing, instead of selling you on more work.
